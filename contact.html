OUR PURPOSE

Restoring Hope. Transforming Lives.

The New Hope Foundation (U) is committed to supporting vulnerable people and communities by promoting compassion, dignity, hope and sustainable development.

ABOUT US

Working Together for a Better Community

The New Hope Foundation (U) is a community-focused organization dedicated to supporting people affected by poverty, disease, famine and inadequate access to basic necessities.

We seek to restore hope, promote human dignity and encourage individuals and communities to become self-reliant and responsible members of society.

Our work is guided by compassion, integrity, accountability and the desire to create lasting positive change.

Our Programs
❤️

Our Commitment

We believe that every person deserves hope, dignity, opportunity and the support necessary to build a better future.

WHO WE ARE

Vision & Mission

👁️

Our Vision

“To reveal the love, hope and compassion of Christ to those affected by poverty, disease, famine and inadequacy of basic needs.”

🤝

Our Mission

“To challenge marginalisation, restore hope, morality and self-consciousness taught by Christ to the African people.”

WHAT WE DO

Our Objectives

01

Fight Poverty

To develop means of fighting and reducing poverty and famine in local communities.

02

Support Vulnerable People

To lobby for support for vulnerable groups and people in dire need.

03

Counselling

To provide basic counselling and support to people experiencing difficult circumstances.

04

Community Development

To promote initiatives that improve the social and economic wellbeing of communities.

OUR WORK

Our Programs

We work with communities to address challenges affecting vulnerable people and promote sustainable development.

🌾

Food Security

Supporting community initiatives that improve food security and livelihoods.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Vulnerable Families

Supporting vulnerable households and individuals in difficult circumstances.

📚

Education

Promoting access to education and opportunities for children and young people.

💼

Livelihoods

Encouraging skills development, entrepreneurship and income-generating activities.

❤️

Counselling & Support

Providing basic counselling, encouragement and community support.

🌍

Community Development

Working with communities to identify challenges and develop sustainable solutions.

WHAT GUIDES US

Our Core Values

01

Compassion

We care about people and respond to human needs with kindness and understanding.

02

Integrity

We strive to act honestly, responsibly and transparently.

03

Accountability

We take responsibility for our actions, resources and commitments.

04

Service

We put the needs and dignity of communities at the centre of our work.

05

Hope

We believe positive change is possible and encourage communities to look toward the future.

06

Teamwork

We collaborate with communities, partners and stakeholders to achieve meaningful results.
